# Promotion and registration plan

**Status:** proposed; no date or channel is confirmed because the college is not named in the supplied brief.

## Audience and message

Invite current students to present or explore practical projects across departments, with faculty as reviewers and campus support teams as invited stakeholders. Keep a separate call for project teams and attendee registration. State the eligibility, proposed format, time limit, venue accessibility, registration deadline, capacity cap, contact route, and photography/recording policy.

## Channels and timing

- T-4 weeks: publish only after date, venue, copy, college branding, and registration form are approved. Use official department channels and campus noticeboards.
- T-3 weeks: faculty announcement and team call through approved student channels.
- T-2 weeks: close team interest, confirm selected teams, and open attendee confirmations up to approved capacity.
- T-1 week: send confirmation and access instructions; open a waitlist if capacity is reached.
- T-1 day: send a reminder with arrival time, venue access, accessibility contact, and recording notice.
- Event day: QR check-in with a paper fallback; reconcile both once.
- T+2 days: send feedback link and thank-you note. Do not publish attendance or outcomes until checked.

## Registration controls

Use a college-approved form and store only the minimum information needed for event updates and check-in. Restrict access to the registration lead. Do not publish the attendee list. Separate event registration from optional photo/video consent. Confirm capacity based on the approved room limit, not registration interest. Provide a waitlist and accessible no-smartphone check-in option.

## Promotion review

Keep a versioned approval copy of poster, caption, registration form, and recording notice. Communications or the authorized branding owner approves college identity and logo use; the faculty coordinator approves event content; the relevant media/privacy authority approves consent language. If any review is pending, do not publish.
