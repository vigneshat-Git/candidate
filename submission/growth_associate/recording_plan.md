# Recording and privacy plan

This is a proposed workflow, not a recording or approval. The event may run fully without video.

## Consent and capture

- Obtain separate opt-in for identifiable capture and for publication; attendance does not imply consent.
- State recording boundaries on the registration page, confirmation, entrance signage, and opening announcement.
- Provide no-camera seating and route; honor verbal or written opt-outs immediately.
- Assign one operator and one consent checker. Capture only approved subjects and clips.
- Use college-approved equipment and storage. Maintain a restricted clip log with clip ID, consent scope, restrictions, editor, and deletion date.

## Shot list

1. Empty room and event sign before attendees enter.
2. Project details, hands, and materials only with team permission.
3. Presenters speaking only after opt-in and approval for publication.
4. Audience wide shot only if every identifiable person in frame has opted in; otherwise use non-identifiable room details.
5. Voluntary short reflections after the session; confirm name/title pronunciation and publication scope.

Never record attendee lists, badge details, private screens/messages, restricted projects, or non-consenting people. Stop immediately on request.

## Editing and release

Keep edits factual; remove incidental non-consenting people, private data, and unapproved IP. Add reviewed captions and accessible text/alt descriptions. Confirm names and project details before publication. The final checklist requires faculty/media approval, consent reconciliation, accessibility review, approved destination, withdrawal/contact route, and retention/deletion date. Unclear scope means do not publish. Retain source footage only for the college-approved period.
