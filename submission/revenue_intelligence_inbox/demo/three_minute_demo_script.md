# Three-minute application demo script

## 0:00–0:30 — Dashboard

Open Overview. “This sample contains 44 distinct sources: 30 calls and 14 meetings. Eighteen have usable text after the update, so transcript coverage is 18/44, or 40.9%. The denominator is the selected source records, not all team activity. Meeting organisers are shown as organisers, not assumed reps.” Click a source-type or person filter, then open the inbox from a metric.

## 0:30–1:10 — Evidence and recommendation

Open M06. Show the analysis and turn 10. “The customer says one expense-recovery invoice has been outstanding for months, while normal service invoices are paid. The cause is still unknown, so I route this for customer follow-up and recommend that the delivery manager investigate. This is an unresolved customer report, not a verified root cause.”

## 1:10–1:45 — Difficult routing decision

Open M07. Show the turn 15 evidence and the attachment issue. “The Review path uploads the document, but it takes extra steps; the direct Recreate payment path still needs investigation. I assign Customer follow-up manually so an active response stays visible, while the analysis preserves the human review route and records why I changed it.” If needed, edit/save the override and show the reason.

## 1:45–2:40 — Replay and persistence

Go to Imports & exports. Reset only before recording the take. Import Initial r1: show 44 records / 17 usable texts. Repeat Initial with reversed order: show 0 added and 44 ignored. In the inbox, open M07 and save the manual Customer follow-up route with a reason. Apply Update r2: show M07 at revision 2, 18 usable texts, and analysis marked Pending review. Replay older Initial r1: show it ignored. Refresh the page and reopen M07: show the updated transcript and manual route remain. Mark the updated analysis reviewed only after showing the pending state.

## 2:40–3:00 — Exports

Open Imports & exports. Download the ZIP and manifest. Show the M07 manifest entry with revision, assigned route, override reason, review state, freshness, and active Markdown path. “Each route has a separate output file, and repeat export creates a fresh archive rather than duplicate active files.”
